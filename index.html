Yuki T. Okamura

Nakanishi lab,
The Institute of Medical Science, The University of Tokyo

Overview

His research broadly focuses on aging, immune regulation, and biomedical science. He is interested in how biological systems change over time and how these changes may contribute to health, disease, and longevity.

Softwares

  • thymus-quant: Python package to measure thymic tissue volume on CT images. It segments the thymic region on CT images and estimates thymic tissue volume.

Works

  • Okamura YT et al. A Novel Feature of Immune Aging. bioRxiv, 2025. A preprint describing immune-cell features associated with aging.
  • Okamura YT et al. Chest CT-based thymic tissue quantification. Annals of Biomedical Engineering, 2025. A study and framework for estimating thymic tissue volume from chest CT images.

Research Interests

Aging biology, immune aging, cellular senescence, translational research, and age-related disease mechanisms.
